# Refund Policy

Acme Support refunds are handled as follows:

- Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of purchase.
- Duplicate charges are refunded in full within **5 business days** after verification.
- Subscription renewals can be refunded if the request arrives within **7 days** of the renewal charge.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method only.
- After 30 days, we offer store credit at support’s discretion — not a cash refund.

How to request: email billing@acme.example with the order id and a short description.
